# Dextop

What if your phone could do more than just connect you to your digital
world? Dextop explores the possibilities of turning your smartphone into
a fully functional desktop computing environment. By bringing desktop
capabilities to a device you already carry, it challenges the
traditional boundaries between mobile and personal computing.

The idea is simple: your workspace, your tools and your digital life,
wherever you go. No bulky hardware, no unnecessary limitations. Just a
new way to think about computing.

**Everything you need, in your pocket. Welcome to the future.**
